About Our Shelter

Paws & Hope Animal Shelter is a registered non-profit organization dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming abandoned, abused, and neglected animals. Our mission is to promote responsible pet ownership and ensure that every animal is treated with compassion and dignity.
